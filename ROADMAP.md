# AudioForge Roadmap

## v1.9.0 completed

- Evidence-qualified room-noise references and conservative propagation.
- Versioned soft-fusion de-esser detector with reproducible generated-corpus metrics.
- Independent dynamics intensity and robust native compressor calibration.
- Guided second-passage native-chain verification with safe rollback.

## Next evidence milestone

- Collect a consented, legally redistributable real-speaker corpus across microphones, rooms, voices, distances, and languages.
- Blind-rate de-esser false activation, missed sibilance, and processing preference.
- Refit or retain the v1 model only when held-out real-speaker calibration and listening evidence materially improves without increasing false activation.

Generated-fixture scores remain engineering regression evidence, not perceptual proof.
